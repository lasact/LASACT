The LACT Program provides the full **180 hours of substance abuse education** required to apply for Counselor-in-Training (CIT) status through the Addictive Disorder Regulatory Authority (ADRA).

We want to make sure you know about something: **LASACT is offering gambling disorder training at no cost to you.**

Tuition is fully covered by the Office of Behavioral Health — which means you get evidence-based, ADRA-approved training without spending a dollar.

---

**Why gambling disorder training?**

Gambling disorder is one of the most underidentified behavioral health conditions in treatment settings. Many clients struggling with substance use also have co-occurring gambling problems that go unaddressed — not because clinicians don't care, but because they haven't had the tools.

This training gives you:
- Evidence-based frameworks for assessing gambling disorder
- Practical treatment approaches you can use immediately
- CEU credit hours through LASACT's AEP status with ADRA

And again — **it's free.** The Office of Behavioral Health has covered the cost.
