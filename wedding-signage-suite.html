07  /  Palette, Materials & Styling Guide

Design Specifications & Vendor Notes — The Boudreauxs · December 20, 2026

Chartreuse Primary

Welcome sign, table numbers (alternating), program background, dance floor perimeter

Olive Mid

Alternate table numbers, seating chart, napkins, velvet tablecloth overlay

Deep Olive / Forest

Menu cards, bar menu accents, program body, floor plan backgrounds

Antique Gold / Brass

Candelabras, charger rims, foil accents on print, napkin rings, frame borders

Signage Materials

  • Welcome sign: frosted or clear acrylic, white/gold 3D raised lettering on easel
  • Programs: 5×7 in. olive card stock, script header, single fold
  • Dinner menus: 4×9 in. deep olive matte card, gold foil title
  • Bar menu: 18×24 in. chartreuse foam board or acrylic on brass easel
  • Table numbers: 5×7 in. alternating chartreuse + olive cards in brass stands
  • Place cards: 3.5×2 in. deep olive, chartreuse left-edge stripe, gold script

Table Styling

  • Chartreuse crushed velvet floor-length cloths on all guest tables
  • Ivory duchess satin runner on sweetheart table
  • Brass/antique gold 5-arm candelabras as primary centerpiece
  • White unscented taper candles throughout
  • White orchids, hydrangeas & trailing smilax at candelabra bases
  • White round charger plates with gold rim

New Orleans Touches

  • Oyster station in outdoor courtyard — raw + chargrilled
  • Beignet dessert station dusted with powdered sugar
  • String quartet for ceremony, live jazz band for reception
  • Sazerac & local craft beer at bar station
  • Café au lait & chicory coffee service after dinner
  • Mini Mardi Gras king cake favors on each place setting

Linen & Napkins

  • Chartreuse crushed velvet tablecloths (floor length)
  • Olive matte satin napkins, bishop's hat fold
  • Napkin ring: thin brass band or olive grosgrain tied with gold twine
  • Cocktail tables: olive linen with chartreuse velvet overlay

Lighting

  • Warm Edison string lights overhead throughout reception hall
  • Chartreuse + warm amber uplighting on wall draping
  • Candlelight primary at all dining tables
  • Sweetheart table flanked by large lanterns with white florals
  • Dance floor: warm amber wash + subtle green accent

Typography

  • Script headlines: Simple Serenity Script (primary preference)
  • Script fallback: Pinyon Script (Google Fonts, loaded in browser)
  • Serif body: Cormorant Garamond Light — refined, editorial
  • Sans labels: Josefin Sans — geometric, minimal caps
  • Print at 300 DPI minimum for all signage
