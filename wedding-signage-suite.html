07  /  Styling Notes & Vendor Checklist

Design Specifications & Recommendations

Signage Materials

  • Welcome sign: acrylic with 3D white raised lettering (match your inspo photo)
  • Programs: 5×7 in. heavyweight ivory cardstock, single fold
  • Menus: 4×9 in. forest green matte card, gold foil title
  • Table numbers: 5×7 in. acrylic or chartreuse cardstock in brass stand
  • Place cards: 3.5×2 in. ivory, chartreuse bottom edge, gold script
  • Bar menu: 18×24 in. foam board or acrylic, mounted on easel

Table Styling

  • Chartreuse crushed velvet floor-length tablecloths on all guest tables
  • Ivory or cream duchess satin runner down sweetheart table
  • Brass/antique gold candelabras (3- or 5-arm) as centerpiece anchors
  • White taper candles — unscented for dinner tables
  • Floral: white orchids, hydrangeas, and trailing greenery at candelabra base
  • White round charger plates with subtle gold rim

Linen & Napkins

  • Chartreuse/olive crushed velvet for reception tables
  • Olive matte satin napkins, folded into bishop's hat (see inspo board)
  • Napkin ring: thin brass ring or olive ribbon tied with gold twine
  • Cocktail tables: ivory linen with chartreuse velvet overlay

Lighting

  • Warm white string lights overhead — bistro or Edison style
  • Candlelight primary at tables — uplighting in chartreuse on draping
  • Sweetheart table: two large floral-encased lanterns flanking the couple
  • Dance floor: soft amber wash lights

Florals

  • White orchids, white hydrangeas, cream roses as dominant blooms
  • Lush trailing greenery (eucalyptus, smilax, ivy)
  • Statement floral arch / backdrop behind sweetheart table
  • Low ground-level florals at ceremony aisle entry

Typography Guide

  • Script (names, headlines): Great Vibes or similar — elegant, flowing
  • Serif (body copy, menus): Cormorant Garamond — light, refined
  • Sans (labels, caps): Josefin Sans — geometric, minimal
  • Print all signage at 300 DPI minimum for crisp reproduction
