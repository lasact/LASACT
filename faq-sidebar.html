Quick Guide

What is asynchronous learning?

Asynchronous learning allows you to learn on your own schedule, within a certain timeframe. You can access and complete lectures, readings, homework, and other learning materials at any time during your course window — no set login times required.

Education vs. supervision hours

Education hours are completed through coursework and training programs like those offered here.

Supervision hours are earned by working in the field under a qualified supervisor — they cannot be obtained through coursework alone.

What is rolling admissions?

We review applications as they arrive, rather than waiting for a fixed deadline — and admission is not binding, so you can still compare financial aid offers from other schools.

Applying early is beneficial. Spots and scholarships fill up quickly, and competition becomes fiercer as time goes on. We accept students immediately upon enrolling.
